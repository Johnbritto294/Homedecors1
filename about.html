Our belief

Furniture shouldn't be anonymous.

Stackly started as a two-person buying trip through Portuguese joinery towns, looking for chairs worth keeping. Ten years later we work with 48 independent workshops across 12 countries, and every product page still names the maker behind it — because a chair is easier to love when you know whose hands built it.

How we got here

A decade, roughly.

2016

Two suitcases and a buying trip

Our founders drove from Porto to the Alentejo sourcing the first eleven pieces, sold out of a shared apartment.

2019

First workshop partnership

A third-generation joinery in Águeda became our first standing production partner, still making Linden frames today.

2022

Carbon-neutral shipping

We moved every order onto offset sea and rail freight, cutting our average delivery emissions by 60%.

2026

48 workshops, 12 countries

The current catalog — 142 pieces, each with a named maker on its product page.
